This mod removes Leaf Litter from world generation.

Instead, you can obtain leaf litter by punching trees, which has a chance of dropping litter around it.

This mod requires [Balm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/balm), which must be installed alongside the mod.

### ![Features](https://mods.twelveiterations.com/img/features-header.png)

- Removes leaf litter from world generation
- Punching trees creates leaf litter around them
- Leaf litter piles up naturally as you keep punching
- Chance for leaf litter being created when punching is configurable
